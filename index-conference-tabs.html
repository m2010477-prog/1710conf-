Textbook Graveyard: Why Emergent Language Deserves More Lesson Time Than Coursebook Units

She also runs teacher development courses and provides methodology consultancy.

Мария Сологуб

From CELTA to SELTA: Why Emotions Shape Learning

Her course on teacher confidence and anxiety management was awarded a Cambridge Distinction.

Алиса Сосонкина

What Is It That Makes the Difference Between Language That Is “Correct” and Language That Feels Natural and Effortless?
